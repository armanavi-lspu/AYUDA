How to Apply for Assistance Programs
Step-by-Step Application Process
  1. Choose a Program Category

    Browse the four main categories above and select the one that best fits your needs: AICS, ESA, 4Ps, or CAL.

  2. Browse Available Programs

    Click "View Programs" to see all available assistance programs within your selected category.

  3. Review Program Requirements

    Carefully read the eligibility criteria, required documents, and program details before applying.

  4. Fill Out Application Form

    Click "Apply for this Program" and complete all required fields accurately and honestly.

  5. Submit Required Documents

    Upload clear copies of all necessary supporting documents as specified in the program requirements.

  6. Wait for Processing

    Your application will be reviewed by MSWD staff. Processing time varies depending on the program and volume of applications.

Important Reminders
  • Ensure all information provided is accurate and truthful
  • Keep copies of all submitted documents for your records
  • Check your application status regularly through your account
  • Respond promptly to any requests for additional information
  • Contact the MSWD Office if you encounter any issues
Eligibility Requirements

Each program has specific eligibility criteria. Please review them carefully to ensure you qualify before submitting your application. Incomplete or ineligible applications may be rejected.
