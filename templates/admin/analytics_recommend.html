About Beneficiary Recommendation

This page generates a prioritized list of beneficiaries for the selected program using profile matching and need-based scoring.

Who Is Included
  • Community users under your assigned municipality ({{ admin_municipality or 'Your municipality scope' }}).
  • Users who match the selected filters, including Family Monthly Income threshold and selected priority barangays.
  • Users ranked by recommendation score/similarity for the selected program.
  • Only users without active application/cooldown restrictions are included in the main list and notification target.
Why Some Users Are Not Included
  • They belong to a different municipality than your admin assignment.
  • They have an active application or are still within an active cooldown period.
  • Their Family Monthly Income is outside the selected income range.
  • They are outside selected priority barangays (when barangays are selected).
  • The recommendation limit is reached (for example, only top Max Beneficiaries are returned).
  • The selected program is not recommendation-eligible (ESA or Emergency programs are excluded).
